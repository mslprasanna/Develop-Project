Cities in India

  1. Andhra Pradesh
    • visakhapatnam
      • Visakhapatnam is a major coastal city in Andhra Pradesh located along the Bay of Bengal. It is known for its beautiful beaches, busy port, and is often called the “City of Destiny.”
    • Vijayawada
      • Vijayawada is a major city in Andhra Pradesh located on the banks of the Krishna River. It is known for the famous Kanaka Durga Temple and is an important business and transportation hub.
    • Guntur
      • Guntur is a major city in Andhra Pradesh known for its large production and trade of chili pepper. It is an important agricultural and commercial center near the Krishna River.
    • Tirupati
      • Tirupati is a famous pilgrimage city in Andhra Pradesh. It is known for the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, one of the most visited temples in the world.
    • Kurnool
      • Kurnool is a historic city in Andhra Pradesh located on the banks of the Tungabhadra River. It once served as the capital of Andhra State from 1953 to 1956.
  2. Karnataka
    • Bengaluru
      • Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka and is famously known as the “Silicon Valley of India” for its booming IT industry. It is also known for its pleasant climate, gardens, and vibrant culture
    • Mysore
      • Mysuru is a historic city in Karnataka, famous for the grand Mysore Palace. It is also known for its Dussehra festival, silk, and sandalwood products
    • Mangaluru
      • Mangaluru is a major port city in Karnataka located on the Arabian Sea coast. It is known for its beaches, seafood, and educational institutions, as well as its rich cultural heritage.
    • Hubballi
      • Hubballi (also called Hubli) is a commercial and industrial city in Karnataka. It is known for being a major trading hub and, together with Dharwad, forms the twin city called Hubballi-Dharwad.
    • Dharwad
      • Dharwad is a city in Karnataka known for its educational institutions and classical music heritage. It forms a twin city with Hubballi, making Hubballi-Dharwad one of the major urban centers in northern Karnataka.
  3. Tamil Nadu
    • Chennai
      • Chennai is the capital city of Tamil Nadu and a major cultural, economic, and educational hub in South India. It is famous for Marina Beach, classical music and dance, and its thriving automobile and IT industries.
    • Coimbatore
      • Coimbatore is a major city in Tamil Nadu, known as the “Manchester of South India” for its textile industry. It is also famous for engineering colleges, IT parks, and pleasant climate.
    • Madurai
      • Madurai is a historic city in Tamil Nadu, famous for the Meenakshi Amman Temple. It is one of the oldest cities in India and is known for its rich culture, temples, and vibrant festivals.
    • Tiruchirappalli
      • Tiruchirappalli (also called Trichy) is a major city in Tamil Nadu. It is famous for the Ranganathaswamy Temple in Srirangam and its rock fort landmark, as well as being an important educational and industrial center.
    • Salem
      • Salem is a city in Tamil Nadu known for its steel production and textile industries. It is also famous for Sangameswarar Temple, Yercaud hill station nearby, and its rich agricultural and industrial activities.
  4. Kerala
    • Thiruvananthapuram
      • Thiruvananthapuram is the capital city of Kerala and an important hub for government, IT, and education. It is famous for the Padmanabhaswamy Temple, beaches, and its scenic backwaters.
    • Kochi
      • Kochi is a major port city in Kerala, often called the “Queen of the Arabian Sea.” It is famous for its backwaters, Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets, and vibrant trade and tourism.
    • Kozhikode
      • Kozhikode (also called Calicut) is a coastal city in Kerala. It is famous for historical trade with Arabs and Europeans, its beaches, and Malabar cuisine..
    • Thrissur
      • Thrissur is a city in Kerala, known as the “Cultural Capital of Kerala.” It is famous for Thrissur Pooram festival, temples, and rich cultural heritage..
    • Alappuzha
      • Alappuzha (also called Alleppey) is a city in Kerala known as the “Venice of the East”. It is famous for its backwaters, houseboats, and picturesque canals, making it a popular tourist destination.
  5. Maharashtra
    • Mumbai
      • Mumbai is the capital city of Maharashtra and the financial and entertainment hub of India. It is famous for Bollywood, the Gateway of India, Marine Drive, and its bustling city life.
    • Pune
      • Pune is a major city in Maharashtra, known as the “Oxford of the East” for its many educational institutions. It is also famous for its IT industry, cultural heritage, and pleasant climate.
    • Nagpur
      • Nagpur is a major city in Maharashtra, famously known as the “Orange City” for its orange production. It is also an important logistics, trade, and transport hub in central India.
    • Nashik
      • Nashik is a city in Maharashtra, famous for its vineyards, wine production, and religious significance. It is also one of the locations of the Kumbh Mela, attracting millions of pilgrims every 12 years.
    • Aurangabad
      • Aurangabad is a historic city in Maharashtra, famous for its Ajanta and Ellora Caves, which are UNESCO World Heritage Sites. It is also known for Bibi Ka Maqbara, often called the “Mini Taj Mahal,” and its rich Mughal-era heritage..
